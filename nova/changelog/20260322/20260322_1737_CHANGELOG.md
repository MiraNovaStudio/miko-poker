# Add knockout animation — cards fly to center, avatar spins off screen — 20260322.1737

## Summary
Added a dramatic knockout animation when a player is eliminated. Their hole cards fly and shrink toward the center of the table (600ms), then the avatar spins rapidly (3-5 rotations) while flying off the screen in the direction of their seat position (1200ms). After both animations complete, the K.O. label appears with the existing shake+scale effect. The exit direction is based on the seat position with slight randomness for variety.

## Files Changed

### Modified
- **miko-ui/src/components/PlayerSlot.ts** — added `playKnockoutAnimation()` method with three phases: card fly-to-center, avatar spin-and-exit, and K.O. label display
- **miko-ui/src/managers/GameManager.ts** — changed `checkForEliminatedPlayers` to call `slot.playKnockoutAnimation()` instead of `slot.showKnockedOutLabel()`
