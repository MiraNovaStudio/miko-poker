# Fix side pot label collapsing during active play — 20260322.1631

## Summary
Fixed the root cause of side pot labels disappearing when a player goes all-in. The `updatePotLabel` method was merging pot layers when `eligible < 2` non-folded players were at that bet level. During active play, this incorrectly collapsed legitimate side pots because other players simply hadn't called yet (not because the pot was uncontested). Changed the merge condition from `eligible < 2` to `eligible === 0` so side pots remain visible while other players still have a chance to act.
