# Separate knockout and game-over sounds — 20260322.1813

## Summary
Split the knockout and game-over sounds into two distinct audio cues:

- **Knockout sound** (`alphix-knock-out.mp3`, renamed from `alphix-game-over-417465.mp3`) — plays at the start of every knockout animation (AI or human)
- **Game-over sound** (`freesound_community-game-over-38511.mp3`) — plays when the game-over screen triggers (player eliminated or victory)

The game-over sound fires 3 seconds after the knockout animation starts (`triggerDeferredGameOver`), so they don't overlap when the human player gets knocked out.

Also removed stale `playGameOverSound` calls from the pot award sections that could never fire (player wasn't in `eliminatedPlayers` set at pot-award time).
