# Play game over sound instead of win sound when player is eliminated — 20260322.0102

## Summary
When the human player is knocked out, the winning hand sound no longer plays. Instead, the game over sound (alphix-game-over-417465.mp3) plays once. The win sound still plays normally when the player is not eliminated.
