# Fix phantom side pots and add AI pot odds override — 20260322.0036

## Summary
Two fixes:

1. **Phantom side pot display**: When a player went all-in and everyone had equal stacks, a small side pot appeared because folded players' blind contributions weren't counted in the main pot calculation. Fixed by checking whether a real side pot exists (someone bet above the all-in level) before showing the Main/Side breakdown.

2. **AI folding with absurd pot odds**: AI players would fold with $13 remaining into a $5000+ pot. Added a pot odds override: if the call amount is less than 15% of the pot, AI always calls instead of folding. This runs before the existing short-handed override.

## Files Changed

### Modified
- **miko-ui/src/managers/GameManager.ts** — Rewrote side pot detection to check for actual side pot conditions (non-all-in player betting above all-in level, or multiple all-in levels); added pot odds override that prevents AI from folding when call/pot ratio is < 15%
