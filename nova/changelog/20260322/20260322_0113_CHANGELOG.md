# Hide uncontested side pots in display — 20260322.0113

## Summary
When two players go all-in for different amounts and no one else is in the hand, a phantom side pot was shown for the excess (which just gets returned). Fixed by counting eligible non-folded players at each pot layer boundary. Layers with fewer than 2 eligible players are merged into the previous layer instead of being displayed as a separate side pot.

### Modified
