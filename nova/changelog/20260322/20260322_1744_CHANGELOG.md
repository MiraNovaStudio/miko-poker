# Fix knockout animation not visible for AI players — 20260322.1744

## Summary
Fixed the knockout animation not playing for AI players who were eliminated at showdown. Three issues prevented the animation from being visible:

1. **Card redeal after animation start**: `checkForEliminatedPlayers` was called BEFORE `revealAllCardsAtShowdown`, so the showdown card reveal would redeal cards and interrupt the card-fly animation. Fixed by reordering: showdown reveals now happen first, then elimination detection.

2. **Card dimming**: `dimNonWinningCards` was setting eliminated players' cards to 25% alpha while the knockout animation was trying to fly them. Added a check to skip eliminated players in the dimming logic.

3. **Showdown hand labels**: `showShowdownHandNames` was adding hand name labels on eliminated players' bet labels during the knockout animation. Added a check to skip players with 0 chips.

## Files Changed

### Modified
- **miko-ui/src/managers/GameManager.ts** — reordered `onHandComplete` so showdown reveals run before elimination detection; added eliminated-player skip in `dimNonWinningCards`; added 0-chips skip in `showShowdownHandNames`
