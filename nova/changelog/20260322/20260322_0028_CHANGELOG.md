# Fix MikoPoker KO victory only triggers when player wins the hand — 20260322.0028

## Summary
Fixed a bug where knocking out MikoPoker by an AI player would incorrectly trigger the "You knocked out MikoPoker!" victory screen. The MikoPoker KO victory now only fires if the human player was one of the hand winners (i.e., the player actually took MikoPoker's chips). If an AI knocks out MikoPoker, it's treated as a normal elimination.

Also fixed the side pot display math (main pot no longer fluctuates) and added the win sound changelog from earlier.

## Files Changed

### Modified
- **miko-ui/src/managers/GameManager.ts** — Added `lastHandWinners` field tracking who won each hand; all four MikoPoker KO victory checks now require `lastHandWinners.includes('player')`; rewrote `updatePotLabel()` to compute main pot directly from all-in level × contributors (locked value) with side pot as remainder
