# Animate folded cards flying to center of table — 20260312.2140

## Summary
When a player folds, their hole cards now animate flying toward the community cards area (center of table) with an ease-in acceleration, shrinking and fading out. AI cards fly as face-down backs, player cards fly face-up — whatever is currently displayed animates. Also moved top-row action label offset from y=75 to y=95.

## Files Changed

### Modified
- **miko-ui/src/components/PlayerSlot.ts** — Added `foldCardsTo(targetX, targetY)` method with 400ms ease-in animation
- **miko-ui/src/managers/GameManager.ts** — Changed fold action from `hideCards()` to `foldCardsTo()` targeting community cards position
- **miko-ui/src/utils/constants.ts** — Top-row bet label offset y: 75 → 95
