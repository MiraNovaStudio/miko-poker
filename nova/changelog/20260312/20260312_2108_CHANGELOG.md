# Fix first-to-act check label being cleared by street change delay — 20260312.2108

## Summary
Fixed bug where the first player to act in a new betting round (e.g., ai3 checking on the flop) would have their action label cleared. The 2-second delayed `clearBet()` in `onStreetChanged` was racing with new actions — bets from the new round were being wiped by the stale clear timer. Fix: clear previous round's bets immediately when the street changes, only delay community card display (1.5s).

## Files Changed

### Modified
- **miko-ui/src/managers/GameManager.ts** — `onStreetChanged()` clears bets immediately instead of on a 2s delay. Community card display delayed 1.5s.
