# Named AI player roster with consistent playstyles — 20260312.2300

## Summary
Added 32 named AI opponents with distinct, consistent playstyles (passive-fish, tight-passive, calling-station, LAG, TAG, maniac, nit, balanced). At least 44% are passive. Each game randomly picks 7 from the roster. When an AI is knocked out, a new player buys in from the remaining roster and is announced. Knocking out all 32 players displays "YOU WIN!" with a Play Again option. Display names are shown everywhere instead of engine IDs (ai1, ai2, etc.).
