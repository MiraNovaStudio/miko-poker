# Deal for the button at game start — 20260312.2340

## Summary
At the start of each new game, one card is dealt face-up to each player to determine who gets the dealer button. Highest card wins (Ace high), with suit tiebreaker: spades > hearts > diamonds > clubs (e.g., 10 of spades beats 10 of diamonds). Cards are dealt with a staggered animation, the winner is announced with their high card, and then the first hand begins with the button at the winning seat.
