# Fix postflop hand strength scale and passive betting — 20260312.2325

## Summary
Fixed the postflop hand strength mapping which was compressing all values into 0-0.25 range (pair=0.125, two pair=0.25). Replaced with a realistic spread: high card=0.10, pair=0.30, two pair=0.50, trips=0.65, straight=0.75, flush=0.80, full house=0.88, quads=0.95, straight flush=1.0. This makes AI thresholds work as intended.

Also fixed passive players never betting made hands — they now bet ~35% of the time with a pair or better (top pair, etc.), and all playstyles bet strong hands (two pair+) at least 60% of the time.

### Modified
- **engine/src/ai/postflop.ts** — `evaluatePostflopHand()`: new category-to-strength mapping with realistic spread; `decidePostflopAction()`: made hands (pair+) trigger bets even for passives at 35% rate; strong hands bet at minimum 60% regardless of aggression config
