# Side Pot Display & Slider Fixes — 20260324.1905

## Summary
Fixed three issues with the side pot display and bet slider:
1. **Slider overflow**: The bet slider panel could go off-screen when the viewport was narrow (e.g., dev tools open). Now clamped to viewport bounds.
2. **Premature side pot amounts**: Side pot amounts were shown in full color before bets were collected from the table. Now shown in gray/dimmed until bets are animated into the pot.
3. **Phantom side pot**: An extra side pot was displayed for uncalled bets (e.g., when one player bet more than anyone else could match). Single-eligible pot layers are now filtered out of the display since they represent uncalled bets that get returned.

## Files Changed

### Modified
- **miko-ui/src/components/ActionBar.ts** — Clamped slider panel position to viewport bounds in `updateInputPosition()`
- **miko-ui/src/components/StatusBar.ts** — Added `dimmed` parameter to `setPotWithDots()` for gray text/dots on uncollected amounts
- **miko-ui/src/managers/GameManager.ts** — Pass `dimmed: true` when showing side pots during active betting; filter out single-eligible pot layers (uncalled bets) from display; added `dimmed` parameter to `updatePotLabel()`

## Test results
- Engine unchanged; UI-only fixes.
