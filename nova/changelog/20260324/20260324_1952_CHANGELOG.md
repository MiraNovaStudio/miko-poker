# Side Pot Fly Animation & All-In Fix — 20260324.1952

## Summary
Three changes:
1. **Side pot chip animation**: Side pots now fly from their label position (Main/Side dot row) to the winner, not from the central chip area. Main pot still flies from the chip area. This makes it visually clear which pot is being awarded.
2. **All-in from slider fix**: Clicking "All In" on the bet slider and pressing Raise now correctly sends an `allin` action when the amount equals remaining chips, preventing the SB from being left behind (e.g., $5 remaining after "all-in").
3. **Full house label wrap**: "Jacks Full of Sevens" now renders on two lines to prevent overflow.

## Files Changed

### Modified
- **miko-ui/src/managers/GameManager.ts** — `animateSinglePotAward` accepts optional `origin` parameter for custom fly position; sequential pot award loop passes side pot label position as origin; `onBetOrRaise` sends `allin` action when amount >= remaining chips; full house hand name wraps to two lines
- **miko-ui/src/components/StatusBar.ts** — Added `getPotLineScenePosition(lineIndex)` and `getPotLineCount()` methods
