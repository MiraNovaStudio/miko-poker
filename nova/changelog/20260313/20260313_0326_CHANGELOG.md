# Add vertical bet slider with pot-fraction buttons — 20260313.0326

## Summary
Added a vertical bet slider panel in the lower right of the screen, inspired by BetOnline's bet controls. The panel appears when bet/raise actions are available and contains a vertical range slider plus stacked pot-fraction buttons (1/4, 1/2, 3/4, Pot, All In). The slider and buttons sync bidirectionally with the bet input field — dragging the slider updates the input, typing in the input moves the slider, and pot-fraction buttons compute the correct raise amount (callAmount + fraction × (pot + callAmount)).

## Files Changed

### Modified
- **miko-ui/src/components/ActionBar.ts** — Added slider panel (HTML overlay) with vertical range input, pot-fraction buttons, and amount label; added `setBetLimits()` method; bidirectional sync between slider, buttons, and bet input; panel shows/hides with action bar
- **miko-ui/src/managers/GameManager.ts** — Calls `actionBar.setBetLimits()` in `advanceGame()` when showing human player controls, passing min bet, max (player chips), pot, and call amount
