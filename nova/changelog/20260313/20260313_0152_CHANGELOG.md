# Fix deferred replacement off-by-one: ensure at least 1 short-handed hand — 20260313.0152

## Summary
Fixed an off-by-one in the deferred replacement delay. The tick-down runs before the next hand starts, so `handsRemaining = 1` would promote the replacement immediately without any short-handed play. Changed minimum from 1 to 2 so the player always plays at least 1 complete hand short-handed before the replacement joins (1-3 hands short-handed).

## Files Changed

### Modified
- **miko-ui/src/managers/GameManager.ts** — Changed deferred replacement `handsRemaining` from `1 + random(0-2)` to `2 + random(0-2)`, ensuring 1-3 hands are played short-handed before fill
