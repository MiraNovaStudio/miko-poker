# Defer game-over modal until after pot award animations — 20260321.1017

## Summary
The rebuy/quit game-over modal was appearing immediately when the player was eliminated, before the pot award animations (side pot → main pot) had finished playing. Now `checkForEliminatedPlayers()` accepts a `deferGameOver` flag — when true, it records eliminations and shows knockout labels but does NOT trigger the game-over modal. A new `triggerDeferredGameOver()` method fires after the award animations complete plus a 3-second pause, giving the player time to see what happened before the modal appears.

## Files Changed

### Modified
- **miko-ui/src/managers/GameManager.ts**
  - `checkForEliminatedPlayers()` — Added `deferGameOver` parameter; when true, skips game-over modal
  - `triggerDeferredGameOver()` — New method that shows the game-over modal after animations finish
  - `onHandComplete()` — Calls `checkForEliminatedPlayers(true)` and schedules `triggerDeferredGameOver()` after `extraDelay + 3000ms`
