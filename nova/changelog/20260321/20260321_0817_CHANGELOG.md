# Fix cards dealt with 50% opacity after peeking at folded cards — 20260321.0817

## Summary
After folding and using the click-to-peek feature to view folded cards at 50% opacity, the next hand's cards would also appear at 50% opacity. The `cardsContainer.alpha` was not being reset to 1 in `resetForNewHand()`.

## Files Changed

### Modified
- **miko-ui/src/components/PlayerSlot.ts** — Added `this.cardsContainer.alpha = 1` in `resetForNewHand()` to restore full opacity
