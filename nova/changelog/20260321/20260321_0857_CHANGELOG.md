# Fix stale Check labels when a raise reopens action — 20260321.0857

## Summary
When the BB checked preflop and a later player raised, the BB's bet label still showed "Check" instead of their blind amount ($10). A raise reopens action for players who already acted, but their stale action labels were never cleared. Now when a raise or bet occurs, all non-folded, non-all-in players' labels are reset to show their current bet amount (or cleared if no bet), so the BB correctly displays their blind amount while awaiting their turn.

### Modified
