# Show pot breakdown during sequential pot awards — 20260321.1039

## Summary
During the pot award ceremony, the pot labels now stay visible and peel off one at a time as each pot is awarded. Previously the labels were hidden immediately and the awards were hard to follow.

New behavior:
1. Full breakdown visible: "Main Pot: $600 / Side Pot 1: $400 / Side Pot 2: $200"
2. Side Pot 2 is awarded → animation flies from label to winner → label updates to "Main Pot: $600 / Side Pot 1: $400"
3. Side Pot 1 is awarded → label updates to "Main Pot: $600"
4. Main Pot is awarded → label clears
