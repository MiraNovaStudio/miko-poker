# Fix side-seat action labels overlapping on turn — 20260321.0754

## Summary
The action labels for ai2 (seat 2) and ai6 (seat 6) were only moving to the lower/wide position on the river (5 cards). They now move to the lower position starting from the turn (4 cards) to avoid overlapping with the community cards.
