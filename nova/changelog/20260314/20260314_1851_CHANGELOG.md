# Move side-seat bet labels based on street — 20260314.1851

## Summary
Seats 2 and 6 (left/right side) now show their action/bet labels in a middle position (toward table center) during preflop, flop, and turn, then shift to the wider below-board position on the river when the 5th community card is out. Labels reset to middle on each new hand.

## Files Changed

### Modified
- **miko-ui/src/components/PlayerSlot.ts** — added `_betOffsetMiddle` and `_betOffsetWide` positions for side seats; added `updateBetLabelForStreet(street)` method; reset to middle in `resetForNewHand()`
- **miko-ui/src/managers/GameManager.ts** — call `updateBetLabelForStreet()` on all slots in `onStreetChanged()` and during all-in runout `processStreetQueue()`
