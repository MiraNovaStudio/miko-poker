# Short-Handed AI Adjustment — 20260314.2243

## Summary
AI players now dynamically adjust their play based on table size. At a full table (6+) they play their normal ranges. As the table shrinks, they loosen up significantly:
- 5 players: VPIP threshold × 0.85
- 4 players: VPIP threshold × 0.70
- 3 players: VPIP threshold × 0.55
- Heads-up (2 players): VPIP threshold × 0.40, aggression +0.25, bluff frequency +0.15

This prevents the unrealistic behavior of AI folding most hands heads-up.
