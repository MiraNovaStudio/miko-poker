# Add hair to avatars — 20260314.1853

## Summary
Added procedural hair to avatar generation. ~75% of avatars now have hair (25% bald). Avatars with hats or headbands skip hair since it would be covered. Five hair styles are available: short cropped, medium side-parted, long flowing, spiky/messy, and curly/afro. Female avatars lean toward longer and curly styles; males lean toward short and spiky. Nine hair colors range from black through blonde to silver and dark red.

## Files Changed

### Modified
- **miko-ui/src/utils/createAvatar.ts** — added `HAIR_COLORS` palette (9 colors); added 5 hair drawing functions (`drawShortHair`, `drawMediumHair`, `drawLongHair`, `drawSpikyHair`, `drawCurlyHair`); integrated hair into `createAvatar()` with gender-weighted style selection, bald probability, and correct draw order (long/curly behind head, others on top)
