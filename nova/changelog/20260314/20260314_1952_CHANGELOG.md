# Distinguish sets from trips, show "Pair of X" — 20260314.1952

## Summary
Updated hand name display to use proper poker terminology. "One Pair" now shows as "Pair of Kings", "Pair of Aces", etc. "Three of a Kind" now distinguishes between a set (pocket pair hits a third on the board) and trips (one in hand, two on board). Sets display as "Set of Sixes", "Set of Jacks", etc. Applies to the live hand label, showdown labels for all players, and the animated winner label.
