# Enlarge avatars and position on stack box — 20260314.1643

## Summary
Made player avatars much larger (80x80 instead of 40x40) and repositioned them to sit on top of the chip overlay/stack box. Avatars are rendered behind the cards container in z-order, so when cards are dealt they cover most of the avatar with just the top of the head peeking out. When a player folds, the avatar becomes fully visible again.
