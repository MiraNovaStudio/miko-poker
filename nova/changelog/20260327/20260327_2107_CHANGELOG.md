# Raise hole cards at showdown so they're visible — 20260327.2107

## Summary
During showdown (especially multi-pot), the WINNER label and chip overlay would cover players' hole cards making them unreadable. Now all revealed hole cards animate upward by 60px at showdown so the card faces are visible above the overlays. Cards reset to their normal position when cleared for the next hand.

## Files Changed

### Modified
- **miko-ui/src/components/PlayerSlot.ts** — Added `raiseCardsForShowdown()` (animated shift up) and `resetCardPosition()` methods; `clearCards()` now resets position automatically
- **miko-ui/src/managers/GameManager.ts** — Called `raiseCardsForShowdown()` in `revealCardsForPlayers()` after dealing showdown cards
