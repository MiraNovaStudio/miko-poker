# Fix missing avatar after rebuy — 20260323.1039

## Summary
After a player was eliminated and used the Rebuy button, their avatar was not restored. The knockout animation destroys the avatar sprite, but the `rebuy()` method did not re-apply it. Added `applyAvatar()` call during rebuy to restore the player's avatar.

## Files Changed

### Modified
- **miko-ui/src/managers/GameManager.ts** — Added `this.applyAvatar(slot, 'player')` in `rebuy()` to restore the human player's avatar after rebuy.

## Test results
- UI-only change; no engine tests affected.
