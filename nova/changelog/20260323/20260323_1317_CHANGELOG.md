# Persist Knocked Out label until next hand — 20260323.1317

## Summary
The "Knocked Out" label on eliminated players was disappearing after 5 seconds due to an auto-hide timer. Changed it to persist until the next hand starts, when `resetForNewHand()` naturally clears it.

## Files Changed

### Modified
- **miko-ui/src/components/PlayerSlot.ts** — Pass duration `0` to `showKnockedOutLabel()` during knockout animation, and skip the auto-hide timer when duration is 0.
