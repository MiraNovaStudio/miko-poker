# Deferred Pot Display with Bet-to-Pot Fly Animation — 20260323.2228

## Summary
Refactored pot display to update only at the end of each betting round instead of after every bet. During active betting, the pot label shows the previous round's total (frozen). When a street changes, bet labels animate flying from player slots into the pot chip area, then the pot label updates to the new total. Side pots are computed and displayed only after betting completes, not in real time as they form. All-in runout and showdown paths manage pot display independently.

## Files Changed

### Modified
- **miko-ui/src/managers/GameManager.ts** — Added `potFrozen`/`frozenPotAmount` state. Modified `updatePotLabel()` to show frozen amount during betting. Removed live pot updates from `pot_updated`, `onBlindPosted`, and `onPlayerAction` handlers. Added `animateBetsToPot()` (500ms fly animation from bet labels to pot chip area) and `unfreezeAndUpdatePot()`. Wired fly animation into `onStreetChanged`. `onHandComplete` unfreezes pot before awards. All-in runout paths unfreeze `potFrozen` since they manage display directly. Reset freeze state in `restart()`.
- **miko-ui/src/components/PlayerSlot.ts** — Added `hasBetAmount` getter to detect numeric bet labels (vs action text like "Check"/"Fold") for the fly animation.
